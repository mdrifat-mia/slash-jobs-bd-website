Common Questions

I found an incorrect job posting

Please let us know by emailing us with details about the posting. We'll investigate and update or remove it as needed.

Can I submit a job posting?

If you represent an organization with an open position, email us with details. We verify all submissions before publishing.

Do you have a partnership program?

We're interested in working with educational institutions, career counselors, and employer partners. Reach out to discuss collaboration opportunities.

I applied through Slash Jobs BD but haven't heard back

Slash Jobs BD facilitates the connection to job postings but is not the employer. Please follow up directly with the company listed in the job posting using the information they provided.

Response Time

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days (Monday-Friday). For urgent matters, please mark your email accordingly.
