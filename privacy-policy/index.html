Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

Slash Jobs BD is a static website platform. We do not collect personal data, use tracking cookies, or run backend servers that store user information. Your privacy is protected by design.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not collect or store your name, email, phone number, or other personal information.
  • We do not use cookies to track your browsing behavior.
  • We do not use analytics services that track individual users.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not have user accounts or login systems.

External Links

Slash Jobs BD provides links to job applications hosted on employer websites. When you click "Apply" or follow a link to an employer's application page, you leave Slash Jobs BD and enter their domain. You are subject to that website's privacy policy, not ours. We recommend reading their privacy policy before submitting personal information.

Browser-Level Processing

The search, filtering, and sorting features on Slash Jobs BD work entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No search queries or filter selections are sent to any server. All processing happens on your device.

Future Services

If Slash Jobs BD introduces new services in the future (such as email job alerts, user accounts, or analytics), we will update this privacy policy accordingly. Any data collection will be optional and clearly disclosed.

Questions?
