Employer Verification

Slash Jobs BD makes reasonable efforts to source jobs from legitimate employers. However:

  • We cannot fully vet every employer or guarantee legitimacy
  • Before applying, research the employer independently using trusted sources
  • Look for red flags: requests for payment, vague job descriptions, pressure to apply quickly
  • If something seems too good to be true, it probably is

If You Suspect Fraud

If you encounter a suspicious job posting or suspect fraud:

  1. Do not send money or personal financial information
  2. Report the posting to the platform it was sourced from (LinkedIn, employer website, etc.)
  3. Contact local authorities if you've been defrauded
  4. Email us at mdrifatmia399@gmail.com with details so we can investigate

Safe Job Searching Tips

  • Research the employer: Check their official website, LinkedIn, and news articles
  • Verify the job: Look for the position on the employer's careers page
  • Never pay upfront: Legitimate employers don't ask for application fees or payment
  • Avoid vague descriptions: Real jobs have specific responsibilities and requirements
  • Check domain names: Ensure application links are from official employer domains
  • Trust your instincts: If something feels wrong, move on to another opportunity
