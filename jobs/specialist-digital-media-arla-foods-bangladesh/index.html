About This Position

Arla Foods Bangladesh is hiring a Specialist – Digital Media & Communication to lead digital media planning, content, campaigns, PR activities, and media engagement.

Job Description

Join Arla Foods Bangladesh as a Specialist – Digital Media & Communication and lead our digital transformation initiatives. You'll manage comprehensive digital campaigns across multiple platforms while driving engagement and brand awareness.

Responsibilities

  • Lead digital media planning, execution, and optimization across Meta, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms
  • Develop platform-relevant digital content aligned with business and brand objectives
  • Manage creative, media buying, PR agencies, business partners, and vendors
  • Support PR campaigns, product launches, corporate events, and media engagement
  • Analyze campaign performance and provide actionable recommendations
  • Collaborate with internal teams to ensure brand consistency

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree from a reputed university
  • 5+ years of experience in digital and offline media
  • Experience with MNCs, local conglomerates, or relevant agencies
  • Strong knowledge of major digital media platforms and current trends
  • Experience managing media agencies and large-scale campaigns
  • Experience writing press releases, media articles, and external publications
  • Excellent written and verbal English communication skills
