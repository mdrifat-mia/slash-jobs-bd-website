About Slash Jobs BD

Curating opportunities for students, graduates, and professionals

Our Mission

Slash Jobs BD is a job discovery media platform dedicated to connecting students, fresh graduates, and early-career professionals in Bangladesh with legitimate, verified job opportunities from credible employers.

We believe in the power of accessible, high-quality job information. By curating opportunities from verified sources and presenting them in a clear, searchable format, we help young professionals take control of their career journey.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Verified Sources: We source opportunities from official job postings, company websites, and LinkedIn, ensuring authenticity.
  • Student-Focused: Our platform is tailored for students and recent graduates looking to start their careers or find internships.
  • Clear Information: Each opportunity includes detailed information about responsibilities, requirements, and how to apply.
  • No Hidden Agendas: We're a transparent platform. We don't hide job details behind walls or misrepresent opportunities.
  • Bangladesh-First: We focus on opportunities in and relevant to Bangladesh, understanding the local job market.

How It Works

  1. Curation: We source job opportunities from official sources and credible employers.
  2. Verification: We verify key details to ensure legitimacy and accuracy.
  3. Organization: Jobs are organized by category, location, experience level, and role type for easy discovery.
  4. Publishing: We publish opportunities with detailed information and direct links to apply with the employer.
  5. Updates: We regularly update our job board as new opportunities become available.

What We're Not

Slash Jobs BD is not:

  • A recruitment agency or employer
  • A resume/CV checking service
  • An interview coaching platform
  • Responsible for hiring decisions
  • A replacement for direct communication with employers

We're a media platform that surfaces opportunities. When you apply, you're applying directly through the employer's official channel.
